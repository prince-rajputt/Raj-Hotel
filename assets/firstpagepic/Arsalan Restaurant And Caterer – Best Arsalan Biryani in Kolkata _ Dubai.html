Welcome to Arsalan Restaurant and Caterer - The Heart of Kolkata’s Biryani Culture

Discover the essence of Mughlai cuisine at Arsalan Restaurant and Caterer, where every bite is a celebration of flavor and tradition. Since our inception on October 6, 2002, with our first branch in Park Circus, Kolkata, we have proudly served food lovers across the city and beyond with our exquisite array of biryanis and Mughlai delicacies.

Savor the Best of Kolkata Biryani

At Arsalan, we specialize in the art of Biryani, offering a diverse selection that includes the legendary Chicken Biryani, flavorful Mutton Biryani, and aromatic Hyderabadi Biryani. Arsalan food menu is a tribute to one of the oldest culinary heritage of India, featuring our renowned Kolkata Chicken Biryani and mutton Biryani, each crafted with the best and some secret ingredients and unique techniques.

Customer

Testimonials

One of the leading names in Biryani in Kolkata, getting a table at Arsalan was a big challenge specially because we visited around Durga Puja time. But we were served our food, we realised the wait was totally worth it. The biryani was fantastic. We took mutton and chicken both varieties and it was well cooked. This was served with basic raita and we relished every morsal of it. We ended our lunch with Phirni that was set in mitti ka kasora (clay pot). The dessert was also pretty decent. We will count Arsalan in one of the best places to have Biryani in Kolkata.
Delhi Fun Dos
Place is beautifully decorated with chandeliers and does give you that royal feel. Atmosphere is 5/5. Their absolute best is the simple Mutton Biryani (384/-). However, do avoid the 'Arsalan Special' Biryani (399/-)because it's extremely spicy and doesn't give the actual Biryani feel, not recommended at all. Staffs are polite and seating arrangements are nice as well. Although, you might have to wait 10-15 minutes during lunch hours due to peak time.
Akash Das
This is a ritual that I follow to have food in Arsalan whenever I come to Kolkata. There was a huge line and decided to sit in the ground floor non ac area. Ordered Laccha Paratha , Mutton Tikiya, Mutton Chaap , Mutton Stew and Double mutton Biryani. Everything was good. Best was the mutton tikiya paired with laccha paratha. Stew was a lil let down and hence the 4 star. Biryani as usual was heavenly.
